Chapter 1

First Login and the Two Messes

Open the app address in any browser (Chrome is recommended). You will see the login screen. Enter the password that was set up for you and click Log in. If you or the Munshi type the wrong password five times, the app locks the login for 60 seconds - wait a minute and try again.

Your hostel has two separate messes. Use the East Wing / West Wing switch at the top-right corner of every page to move between them. Each mess keeps its own attendance, ingredients, expenses, rates and reports - switching never mixes the two accounts. The choice is remembered on your phone or computer.

Do this first

Go to Settings and fill in the Resident Tutor's name, the Mess Incharge and Munshi names for both messes, the total students in each mess and the default rate. These details appear on every report, so set them before printing anything.

Change the password

Settings > Change password. Enter the current password once and the new password twice (at least 8 characters). Everyone who uses the app shares this one password, so share it only with the Munshi.

Chapter 2

How to Add a Day (Daily Entry)

Daily Entry is the screen you will use every day, separately for each mess. Make sure the correct mess is selected, then follow these steps:

  1. Pick the date at the top. Today's date opens by default; you can back-date or forward-date freely.
  2. Lunch: type the menu (e.g. "Chicken Karahi + Roti"), the number of students who ate, and the rate per student. The rate is pre-filled with the last rate you used for that mess.
  3. Ingredients used: click Add item for every item. Choose a category, type the item name, quantity, unit and unit price. The Amount column calculates itself (quantity × unit price) and the meal totals update live while you type.
  4. Dinner: repeat the same three fields and its own ingredient list.
  5. Extra expenses: add anything that is not an ingredient - gas cylinders, salaries, repairs, transport - with a category, description and amount. Extras belong to the whole day, not to one meal.
  6. Save day: the green button on the bottom bar stores everything. A confirmation appears when the day is saved.

Save typing with "Copy items from previous day"

This button copies the previous day's menus and ingredient names (with unit prices and vendors) into today's form. Quantities and attendance stay blank because they change every day - just fill them in and save.

Warnings you will see

The app warns you when a meal has ingredients but zero students, when a quantity is entered without a unit price, or when an item has no name. Items without a name are never saved, so nothing incomplete can hide inside the register.

The bottom bar always shows the live day totals: amount at rate, ingredients cost, extras, cost per student-meal and the day's surplus or deficit.

Chapter 3

Fixing Mistakes (Records)

Open Records to see every recorded day of the selected month for the selected mess. Each row shows both meals, the extras and the day's total expenditure.

  1. Edit opens that day in Daily Entry with everything filled in - change anything and press Save day.
  2. Delete removes the whole day (both meals, all ingredients and the extras). The app asks you to confirm before deleting. Deletion is permanent, so keep backups.
Chapter 4

The Weekly Report for the Resident Tutor

The Short Report page produces the one-page summary you currently write by hand. Choose Daily or Weekly (Mon-Sun), pick a date (for a weekly report, any date inside that week), and the report appears on screen with exactly the same numbers that will be printed:

  1. Download PDF - the official one-page document: attendance and price summary on top, then lunch ingredients, dinner ingredients, extra expenses and the overall surplus or deficit. Hand it to the RT or print it.
  2. Download Excel - the same report as a spreadsheet if the RT prefers Excel.
  3. Print - prints the on-screen report directly from the browser.

The Reports page builds the detailed report for a day, a week, a month or any custom date range. It adds the day-wise summary, category-wise expenditure with percentage shares, item-wise consolidated usage, every single ingredient row of every meal, and a signature block for the Mess Incharge, Munshi and Resident Tutor. Download it as PDF or Excel from the buttons at the top.

What the numbers mean

Amount at rate = students who ate × rate per student. Total expenditure = ingredients + extra expenses. Surplus / (Deficit) = amount at rate − total expenditure. Deficits are shown in red with brackets, e.g. (1,581.10).

Chapter 5

Dashboard and Settings

The Dashboard shows the selected month at a glance: total expenditure, amount at rate, surplus or deficit, days recorded, student-meals served and average cost per student-meal, plus a daily line chart, category-wise spending and the top ten items. Use it in meetings to explain where the money went.

Settings has two parts. This mess (switch between East and West at the top of the page) holds the mess display name, Mess Incharge, Munshi, total students and default rate. Common settings are shared by both messes: hostel name, the hostel line under it, the Resident Tutor's name, and the lists of item categories, expense categories and units - you can add or remove entries with one click.

Chapter 6

Back Up Your Data (Very Important)

All data lives in one file on your PC. Backups protect you from disk failure and let you move the data to another computer. Take a backup at least once a week - it takes five seconds.

  1. Open Settings > Data and click Download full backup (JSON). Save the file somewhere safe - WhatsApp "Saved Messages", Google Drive or a USB drive.
  2. To move or recover, click Restore from backup file, choose the backup, and confirm. Restoring replaces everything currently in the app (both messes) with the backup.
  3. Export all data to Excel gives you a human-readable copy of every meal, ingredient and expense for both messes.

Extra safety copy

Once a month, copy the whole app folder (or at least the db folder) to a USB drive while the app is closed. Two copies of data is the golden rule.

Chapter 7

Running It on Your Own PC + Hostel WiFi

The app runs on your own computer; phones open it over the hostel WiFi. One-time setup takes about 15 minutes:

  1. Install Node.js (LTS version) from nodejs.org - keep clicking Next until it finishes.
  2. Unzip the app folder, for example to C:\mess-manager.
  3. Open Command Prompt in that folder (type cmd in the File Explorer address bar and press Enter).
  4. Type npm install and wait (5-10 minutes, once only).
  5. Type npm run build and wait for it to finish (once only, or after updates).
  6. Type npm start. You will see "Ready" - the app is now running on this PC.
  7. Allow it through the firewall: the first time, Windows asks "Allow Node.js...?" - click Allow access (tick both Private and Public if asked).
  8. Find your PC's address: type ipconfig and note the "IPv4 Address" (e.g. 192.168.1.50).
  9. On any phone or laptop connected to the same WiFi, open http://192.168.1.50:3000 (use your own IPv4 number). Bookmark it.

Next time, you only need step 6: open Command Prompt in the folder, run npm start, and leave the window open while the mess is working. Closing the window or putting the PC to sleep takes the app offline until you start it again.

Start automatically (optional)

Double-click start.bat in the app folder - it installs missing pieces, builds if needed and starts the app, then shows the address to open on your phone. To go further, use Windows Task Scheduler to run it at log-on.

Forgotten password

Stop the app, open Command Prompt in the folder and run npm run reset-password. This restores the original password; log in and change it in Settings straight away.

Chapter 8

Questions and Answers

QuestionAnswer
Do the two messes mix anywhere?No. Every page, total, chart and report belongs to the mess selected in the switcher. The Excel export and backup label each row with its mess.
Can I edit a day from last month?Yes - Records, change the month, open the day with Edit, fix it and save. Reports update instantly.
What if the internet is down?Nothing breaks. The app runs on your PC; the WiFi only carries traffic between your devices. No internet connection is needed.
Is anyone else able to open it?Only people on the same WiFi who know the password. The login locks for 60 seconds after 5 wrong attempts, and the password is stored only as an irreversible salted hash.
The phone shows "This site can't be reached".The app is not running (start it with npm start), the PC is asleep, or the phone is on a different WiFi / using mobile data.
Can I move everything to a new PC?Install Node.js, unzip the app, run npm install and npm run build, then restore your latest backup file inside the app.
Sample data is showing - how do I start fresh?Settings > Data > Clear all mess data, type DELETE to confirm, then enter your real days. Settings and the password are kept.
